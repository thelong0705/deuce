we will have 3 roles
- booking users
- court owner users
- A booking user can not be also court owner

For booking users
- sign up / login / logout
- can search for the tennis based on filter
- can book the court in 60 mins block in advance (before the timeslot)
- double book is not allowed
- view my bookings
- cancel booking (1 days advance)

For court owner
- login / logout
- Regist the venue
- Regist the court and its availability
- View bookings on his court
- Block a court for maintenance

Business rules
- If the court block then cancel the slot and notify the users
- Players can book any slot up to two weeks ahead, right up until it starts.
- Bookable from now until two weeks ahead.
